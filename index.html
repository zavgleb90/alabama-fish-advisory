SCIENTIFIC FOUNDATION

Transparent methods

The tool combines real historical total-mercury measurements with the EPA noncancer consumption-rate formula. The calculator is exploratory: official advisories, other contaminants, and location-specific exposure review are not integrated.

✚
HEALTH & SAFETY

Plain-language guidance

People who are pregnant, planning pregnancy, breastfeeding, or feeding children need special attention. The first public release must explain portions, give warnings clearly, and never label unsampled fish as safe.
