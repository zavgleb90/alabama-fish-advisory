SCIENTIFIC FOUNDATION

Transparent methods

The demo uses the EPA noncancer consumption-rate framework for methylmercury. When data arrive, measured tissue concentrations, official advisories, multiple contaminants, and validated geographic estimates must be considered before issuing any recommendation.

HEALTH & SAFETY

Plain-language guidance

People who are pregnant, planning pregnancy, breastfeeding, or feeding children need special attention. The first public release must explain portions, give warnings clearly, and never label unsampled fish as safe.
